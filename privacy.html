Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2025

AURUM (Pty) Ltd ("AURUM", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

AURUM (Pty) Ltd is a responsible party as defined by POPIA. We are registered in South Africa. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@aurumworld.co.za.

2. What personal information we collect

We only collect information that is necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide a service:

  • Name and email address (via our contact and newsletter forms)
  • Phone number, if you choose to provide it
  • Details of your product interest or enquiry
  • Basic web analytics (page views, referring URLs) — no personally identifiable data

We do not collect payment card data. We do not use tracking pixels or third-party advertising cookies.

3. Why we collect it (lawful basis)

  • To respond to enquiries — your consent, given when you submit a form
  • Newsletter — your explicit opt-in consent
  • Legal obligations — where required by South African law

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for a maximum of 3 years. Newsletter subscriber records are kept until you unsubscribe. You may request deletion at any time (see section 7).

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Formspree — our form submission processor (they do not store submissions beyond delivery)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities — only where required by law

6. International transfers

If personal information is processed outside South Africa (e.g., by Formspree servers located abroad), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by POPIA section 72.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa)

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@aurumworld.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies beyond those strictly necessary for basic functionality (no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies). No cookie banner is required.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption and access controls. No internet transmission is 100% secure; we will notify you promptly if a breach affects your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of this website after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@aurumworld.co.za
Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za
